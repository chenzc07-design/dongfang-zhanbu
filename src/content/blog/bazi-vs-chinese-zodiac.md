"I'm a Horse." "I'm a Dragon." "We're not compatible."

Most people treat Chinese zodiac signs like Western sun signs — a single label that defines personality and compatibility. But in traditional Chinese metaphysics, your zodiac animal is just **one of eight characters** in your BaZi chart. It's the year pillar's branch — important, but far from the whole picture.

---

## The Scope Difference

**Chinese zodiac**: Based on your birth YEAR only. 12 animals. Simple, accessible, limited.
**BaZi**: Based on your YEAR, MONTH, DAY, and HOUR. 8 characters, 10 Heavenly Stems, 12 Earthly Branches, 5 Elements. Complex, deep, highly personalized.

Your zodiac sign is like knowing someone's country. Your BaZi chart is like knowing their street address, their house, their room.

---

## Why Your Zodiac Sign Alone Is Misleading

Two people born in the same year have the same zodiac sign. But one born in January and one born in December can have completely different charts — different Day Masters, different elements, different life paths.

I once read charts for two Horses born in the same year. One was a successful CEO who thrived on pressure. The other was a quiet artist who preferred solitude. Same year. Same zodiac sign. Radically different BaZi charts. The difference? Their birth months and hours.

---

## The Accuracy Gap

A zodiac-only reading is about 8% accurate — because the year pillar is only one of four pillars. A full BaZi reading covers all 8 characters and their interactions. That's the difference between a horoscope column and a personalized life map.

**If you only know your zodiac sign, you know almost nothing about your Chinese astrology.**
