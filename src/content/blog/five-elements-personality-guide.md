I looked at his chart: strong Fire. Strong Metal. The man was a blade heated in a forge — sharp, hot, fast. His team? Mostly Earth and Water people — steady, reflective, methodical.

He wasn't working with bad people. He was working with different *elements*.

## The Five Elements: Not Just Philosophy

In Chinese metaphysics, the Five Elements (五行, Wu Xing) are not "things." They're *processes*. Wood isn't a tree — it's the energy of growth, expansion, upward movement. Fire isn't a flame — it's the energy of transformation, heat, radiance.

Every person has all five elements in their chart. But the balance — which elements are strong, which are weak, which are missing — creates your unique elemental fingerprint.

Here's what each element actually means in terms of personality and behavior. Not the textbook version. The version I've confirmed across thousands of readings.

---

## Wood (木) — The Expander

Wood energy is forward motion. Growth. Ambition. It's the force that pushes a seedling through concrete.

**How to spot Wood-dominant people:**
- They're always working on something new
- They get restless without a project
- They're optimists — sometimes to a fault
- They have strong opinions about personal development

**Their superpower**: Wood people *start things*. If you need momentum, find a Wood person.

**Their shadow**: Wood without Water (nourishment) or Earth (grounding) becomes scattered — lots of seeds, nothing fully grown. They also tend to bulldoze over people without noticing.

**In the workplace**: Give Wood people ownership. Don't micromanage them. They'll grow in whatever direction you point them — just make sure it's the right direction.

---

## Fire (火) — The Transformer

Fire energy is warmth, visibility, and transformation. It's the force that turns raw food into nourishment and raw ideas into movements.

**How to spot Fire-dominant people:**
- They're expressive — you always know how they feel
- They're natural performers, even in meetings
- They make decisions based on passion, not spreadsheets
- They light up rooms (and occasionally burn them down)

**Their superpower**: Fire people *inspire*. They make others believe things are possible.

**Their shadow**: Fire without Wood (fuel) burns out fast. Fire without Water (control) becomes destructive. These are the people who start with a blaze of glory and disappear three months later.

**In the workplace**: Give Fire people visibility. Recognition matters more to them than to any other element. A Fire person who feels unseen will leave — not for more money, but for more attention.

---

## Earth (土) — The Stabilizer

Earth energy is stability, nourishment, and patience. It's the soil that holds everything together.

**How to spot Earth-dominant people:**
- They're reliable — almost to a fault
- They don't get excited easily, and they don't panic easily
- They're the person everyone goes to for practical advice
- They build slowly and maintain what they build

**Their superpower**: Earth people *sustain*. They're the reason companies don't fall apart.

**Their shadow**: Earth without Wood (growth) becomes stagnant. Earth without movement becomes stubborn — the immovable object that refuses to budge even when it should.

**In the workplace**: Give Earth people systems to manage. They thrive on routine and reliability. Don't ask them to innovate wildly — ask them to make things work *better*.

---

## Metal (金) — The Refiner

Metal energy is precision, structure, and discernment. It's the force that separates gold from ore, truth from nonsense.

**How to spot Metal-dominant people:**
- They're direct — sometimes brutally so
- They have strong opinions about quality
- They're disciplined and expect others to be
- They notice what's wrong before they notice what's right

**Their superpower**: Metal people *improve things*. They're the editors, the quality controllers, the ones who turn "good" into "great."

**Their shadow**: Metal without Water (flexibility) becomes rigid. Metal without Earth (grounding) becomes cutting for the sake of cutting — criticism without purpose.

**In the workplace**: Give Metal people standards to enforce. They're your best auditors, editors, and quality managers. Just warn their colleagues that "it's not personal."

---

## Water (水) — The Connector

Water energy is flow, wisdom, and adaptability. It's the force that finds its way around every obstacle.

**How to spot Water-dominant people:**
- They're curious about everything
- They connect seemingly unrelated ideas
- They're comfortable with ambiguity
- They communicate well — sometimes too well (they'll talk to anyone)

**Their superpower**: Water people *adapt*. They're the survivors, the navigators, the ones who figure things out on the fly.

**Their shadow**: Water without Earth (containment) floods — too many ideas, no execution. Water without Wood (direction) drifts — talented but aimless.

**In the workplace**: Give Water people problems to solve, not procedures to follow. They need freedom to flow. Put them in a rigid structure and they'll either leave or check out mentally.

## How the Elements Interact

Here's where it gets practical. The elements don't exist in isolation. They interact through two main cycles:

**The Generating Cycle (相生)**:
Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood

Each element feeds the next. If you're a Fire-dominant person working with a Wood-dominant boss, you'll probably get along well — they provide the fuel you need.

**The Controlling Cycle (相克)**:
Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood

Each element restrains another. If you're a Wood person working for a Metal boss... you might feel like you're constantly being cut down. You're not paranoid. It's the elements.

## Why This Matters for Your Life

Here's what I tell every client:

**1. Your dominant element is not your identity — it's your default strategy.**

A Fire-dominant person *can* be methodical and patient. It just takes more energy. Knowing your element helps you understand why some things feel effortless and others feel like swimming upstream.

**2. You need all five elements.**

A chart with only Fire is a wildfire. A chart with only Earth is a desert. Balance isn't about being equal in everything — it's about having *enough* of what you need.

**3. Relationships are elemental chemistry.**

That coworker who drives you crazy? Check their dominant element against yours. It's probably in the Controlling Cycle. Understanding this doesn't fix the relationship — but it takes the personal sting out of it.
