In BaZi, your Day Master is the character on top of your day pillar. It's the most important character in your entire chart — the core of who you are, the operating system you were born with.

There are 10 Day Masters, corresponding to the 10 Heavenly Stems. Five elements, each with a Yang (firm, outward, active) and Yin (yielding, inward, receptive) version. Together, they form 10 distinct archetypes — 10 ways of being human.

Over 25 years of reading charts, I've seen these 10 archetypes play out thousands of times. Each is recognizable. Each has its strengths and its shadow. None is "better" than another — they just are what they are.

Here's a complete guide to all 10 Day Masters, written for you to find yours.

---

## The 5 Yang Day Masters

The Yang stems are firm, active, outward. They move toward the world.

### 1. Jia Wood (甲木) — The Towering Tree

**Element**: Yang Wood
**Symbol**: A tall, sturdy tree — the oak, the pine, the redwood
**Season**: Spring

Jia Wood people are the natural builders. They have vision and patience in equal measure. They start things slowly and finish them, like a tree that grows for a hundred years.

**Strengths**: Leadership, perseverance, vision, integrity
**Shadow**: Stubbornness, inflexibility, slowness to adapt
**Best careers**: Entrepreneurship, architecture, education, government, forestry
**Relationship style**: Loyal but sometimes distant; needs a partner who appreciates quiet strength
**Famous archetypes**: Founders, visionaries, long-term investors

I had a Jia Wood client who spent 12 years building a single business. His friends thought he was slow. By year 15, he was the only one in his industry still standing. Jia Wood doesn't compete on speed.

### 2. Bing Fire (丙火) — The Radiant Sun

**Element**: Yang Fire
**Symbol**: The midday sun
**Season**: Summer

Bing Fire is the sun itself — visible, warm, impossible to ignore. These are the people who light up rooms. They have presence, energy, and a magnetism that draws others in.

**Strengths**: Charisma, confidence, inspiration, warmth
**Shadow**: Burnout, self-centeredness, difficulty with subtlety
**Best careers**: Entertainment, public speaking, leadership, media, marketing
**Relationship style**: Romantic, generous, sometimes overwhelming; needs a partner who isn't threatened by their radiance
**Famous archetypes**: Performers, motivational speakers, founders who love the spotlight

One Bing Fire client became the face of a major brand. Not the CEO — the *face*. He made every interview, every campaign, every public appearance. The company was his vehicle for being seen. He was extraordinary at it.

### 3. Wu Earth (戊土) — The Enduring Mountain

**Element**: Yang Earth
**Symbol**: A massive mountain
**Season**: Between seasons (the "long summer")

Wu Earth is the mountain. Immovable. Reliable. Solid. These people don't bend with every wind. They have a core of stability that others can lean on.

**Strengths**: Reliability, patience, endurance, trustworthiness
**Shadow**: Stubbornness, resistance to change, heaviness
**Best careers**: Real estate, finance, operations, project management, agriculture, insurance
**Relationship style**: Steady, dependable, slow to commit but loyal once committed
**Famous archetypes**: Bankers, real estate moguls, military leaders

A Wu Earth client ran a 200-person company for 25 years. Same industry, same city. He wasn't ambitious in the conventional sense — he just kept his mountain where it was and let people come to him. They did.

### 4. Geng Metal (庚金) — The Tempered Blade

**Element**: Yang Metal
**Symbol**: A sword, freshly forged
**Season**: Autumn

Geng Metal is sharp. Direct. Decisive. These are the people who make the hard calls. They have a clarity of thought that cuts through confusion.

**Strengths**: Decisiveness, clarity, courage, directness
**Shadow**: Harshness, impatience, cutting without thinking
**Best careers**: Surgery, law, editing, military, competitive sports, leadership in crisis
**Relationship style**: Honest to a fault, sometimes hurtful; needs a partner who can take truth without breaking
**Famous archetypes**: Surgeons, judges, editors, founders who pivot hard

Geng Metal clients don't want hand-holding. They want the facts. One Geng Metal executive told me, "I don't pay you to tell me I'm a good person. I pay you to tell me when to move." I gave him the dates. He executed.

### 5. Ren Water (壬水) — The Flowing River

**Element**: Yang Water
**Symbol**: A great river, flowing to the sea
**Season**: Winter

Ren Water moves. It adapts. It finds its way around every obstacle. These are the natural navigators of the world — they thrive in change where others fear it.

**Strengths**: Adaptability, intelligence, wisdom, flexibility
**Shadow**: Restlessness, indecisiveness, difficulty committing
**Best careers**: International business, logistics, journalism, sales, trade, anything that involves movement or change
**Relationship style**: Exciting, unpredictable, sometimes unreliable; needs a partner who can handle their flow
**Famous archetypes**: Diplomats, international traders, founders who build across borders

A Ren Water client of mine has lived in six countries in 20 years. His career has spanned journalism, trade, and now a tech startup. By traditional standards, he's "unstable." By Ren Water standards, he's exactly what he should be — a river finding its way to the sea.

## The 5 Yin Day Masters

The Yin stems are receptive, inward, refined. They move through influence rather than force.

### 6. Yi Wood (乙木) — The Climbing Vine

**Element**: Yin Wood
**Symbol**: A vine, climbing toward light
**Season**: Late spring

Yi Wood is flexible, charming, and surprisingly durable. Vines don't have the strength of trees, but they find their way through cracks that nothing else can penetrate.

**Strengths**: Adaptability, charm, networking, persuasion
**Shadow**: Dependency, people-pleasing, difficulty standing alone
**Best careers**: Sales, marketing, PR, therapy, teaching young children, any field that requires influence
**Relationship style**: Attentive, accommodating, sometimes too eager to please
**Famous archetypes**: Networkers, persuaders, behind-the-scenes influencers

Yi Wood clients are the best connectors I know. They remember birthdays, send thank-you notes, and somehow know the perfect person for every problem. One Yi Wood client built a six-figure consulting business on relationships alone — never spent a dollar on marketing.

### 7. Ding Fire (丁火) — The Steadfast Candle

**Element**: Yin Fire
**Symbol**: A candle flame
**Season**: Late summer

Ding Fire is the flame that burns through the night. Quiet, focused, unwavering. Not the sun that lights the world — the candle that guides one person home.

**Strengths**: Focus, depth, warmth, perseverance
**Shadow**: Burnout, isolation, working too hard alone
**Best careers**: Research, writing, law, accounting, medicine, programming, any field requiring deep concentration
**Relationship style**: Devoted, intense, sometimes smothering; needs a partner who values depth over breadth
**Famous archetypes**: Researchers, deep specialists, founders who build slowly but solidly

Ding Fire clients are the ones who *follow through*. When I tell them to wait six months, they wait. One client spent three years quietly building a niche app, never marketed it, and now supports himself fully from word-of-mouth growth.

### 8. Ji Earth (己土) — The Fertile Garden

**Element**: Yin Earth
**Symbol**: Cultivated farmland
**Season**: Between seasons

Ji Earth is the soil that grows things. Nurturing, patient, productive. These people are the glue of every organization they're part of.

**Strengths**: Nurturing, reliability, productivity, support
**Shadow**: Self-sacrifice, over-giving, difficulty saying no
**Best careers**: HR, education, healthcare, community work, hospitality, any field that nourishes others
**Relationship style**: Deeply caring, sometimes smothering; needs a partner who gives back
**Famous archetypes**: Teachers, HR leaders, community builders

Ji Earth clients are the ones everyone leans on. And they let themselves be leaned on. One Ji Earth office manager at a tech startup was underpaid and overlooked — until she quit. Within three months, the CEO called begging her to return. She did, at double the salary.

### 9. Xin Metal (辛金) — The Polished Jewel

**Element**: Yin Metal
**Symbol**: A finely cut diamond or jewel
**Season**: Late autumn

Xin Metal is refinement itself. Taste, judgment, discernment. These people can tell the difference between good and great at a glance.

**Strengths**: Taste, refinement, precision, discernment
**Shadow**: Perfectionism, snobbery, paralysis from high standards
**Best careers**: Design, fashion, art curation, luxury, consulting, quality assurance, criticism
**Relationship style**: Discerning, particular, hard to please but deeply loyal once committed
**Famous archetypes**: Designers, art directors, founders of premium brands

Xin Metal clients have the most interesting careers because they refuse to do ugly work. One Xin Metal graphic designer quit four agencies in a row. "The work had no soul." She now runs her own studio with a six-month waiting list.

### 10. Gui Water (癸水) — The Morning Dew

**Element**: Yin Water
**Symbol**: Morning dew, gentle rain
**Season**: Late winter

Gui Water is intuition. Quiet knowing. The kind of wisdom that doesn't explain itself.

**Strengths**: Intuition, subtlety, depth, gentleness
**Shadow**: Over-thinking, second-guessing, difficulty asserting
**Best careers**: Psychology, research, strategy, writing, music, spiritual guidance, any field requiring deep perception
**Relationship style**: Sensitive, deep, sometimes withdrawn; needs a partner who appreciates quiet depth
**Famous archetypes**: Investors with inexplicable track records, intuitive therapists, artist-founders

Gui Water clients are the hardest to read but the most accurate readers of others. One Gui Water investor has outperformed every quant fund I know. He doesn't analyze spreadsheets. He meets the founder, sits in silence, and decides.

## How to Find Your Day Master

You need your full birth chart. Calculate it with your birth year, month, day, and hour. The character on top of the day pillar is your Day Master.

The 10 Day Masters are not a personality test. They're deeper — a way of seeing yourself that includes your strengths, your shadow, and your path. Knowing your Day Master doesn't lock you in. It shows you the road you were born to walk.
