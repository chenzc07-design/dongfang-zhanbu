One of the most practical applications of BaZi is health. The Five Elements correspond directly to organs and body systems. When your elements are imbalanced, your body tells you.

---

## Element-Body Map

**Wood (木)** — Liver, gallbladder, eyes, tendons, nerves
- *Excess Wood*: Anger, headaches, eye strain, muscle tension
- *Weak Wood*: Fatigue, poor vision, indecision, joint stiffness

**Fire (火)** — Heart, small intestine, blood, circulation
- *Excess Fire*: Insomnia, anxiety, high blood pressure, skin rashes
- *Weak Fire*: Cold hands/feet, poor circulation, lethargy, depression

**Earth (土)** — Spleen, stomach, muscles, mouth, skin
- *Excess Earth*: Weight gain, sluggish digestion, worry, overthinking
- *Weak Earth*: Poor appetite, weak digestion, fatigue after eating

**Metal (金)** — Lungs, large intestine, nose, skin
- *Excess Metal*: Asthma, skin problems, grief, rigidity
- *Weak Metal*: Weak immunity, frequent colds, low vitality

**Water (水)** — Kidneys, bladder, bones, ears, teeth
- *Excess Water*: Kidney issues, fluid retention, fearfulness
- *Weak Water*: Back pain, weak bones, hearing problems, low willpower

---

## What to Do About Imbalances

**If Wood is weak**: Eat more greens, exercise gently, spend time in nature
**If Fire is weak**: Warm foods, sunlight, cardiovascular exercise
**If Earth is weak**: Stable meal times, root vegetables, grounding practices
**If Metal is weak**: Breathing exercises, white foods, morning routines
**If Water is weak**: Rest, hydration, salt, kidney-supporting foods

BaZi doesn't replace medicine. But it gives you a map of your body's tendencies — a head start on prevention.
