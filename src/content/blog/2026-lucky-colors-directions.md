## 2026 Fire Horse — The Dominant Elements

The year's Stem is Bing Fire (丙). The Branch is Wu Horse (午). The dominant element is **Fire**.

**What this means**: Red, orange, purple, and pink are powerful this year. These are Fire colors.

**Supporting element**: Wood. Wood feeds Fire. Green, brown, and earth tones support the year's energy.

**Weak element**: Water. Water controls Fire. Black and dark blue are less favorable in 2026.

**Clashing element**: Metal. Fire controls Metal. White, gold, silver are neutral to slightly challenging.
