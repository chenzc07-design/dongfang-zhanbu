After 25 years of reading charts, I've noticed a clear pattern: people who work *with* their element thrive. People who fight it burn out.

---

## Wood Element Careers (Jia + Yi Wood)

**Natural strengths**: Growth, expansion, vision, patience
**Best fields**: Entrepreneurship, architecture, education, management, forestry, publishing
**Avoid**: High-pressure sales, rapid-pace environments, micro-management

**Tip**: Wood grows slowly but surely. Don't expect overnight success. Plant seeds, water them, wait.

---

## Fire Element Careers (Bing + Ding Fire)

**Natural strengths**: Visibility, communication, passion, inspiration
**Best fields**: Entertainment, marketing, public speaking, media, coaching, design
**Avoid**: Isolated work, repetitive tasks, roles without recognition

**Tip**: Fire needs fuel. Surround yourself with Wood element people who support your growth.

---

## Earth Element Careers (Wu + Ji Earth)

**Natural strengths**: Stability, reliability, patience, nurturing
**Best fields**: Real estate, finance, HR, education, healthcare, operations
**Avoid**: Chaotic startups, roles requiring constant pivoting

**Tip**: Earth people are the foundation of every organization. Your value is stability. Don't let anyone tell you that's boring.

---

## Metal Element Careers (Geng + Xin Metal)

**Natural strengths**: Precision, discipline, quality, discernment
**Best fields**: Law, surgery, editing, quality control, finance, cybersecurity
**Avoid**: Roles requiring constant social interaction, ambiguous work

**Tip**: Metal cuts through confusion. You're the editor, the auditor, the one who makes things better. Lean into precision.

---

## Water Element Careers (Ren + Gui Water)

**Natural strengths**: Adaptability, wisdom, communication, intuition
**Best fields**: International business, journalism, psychology, research, logistics, trade
**Avoid**: Rigid corporate structures, roles without autonomy

**Tip**: Water flows. If you feel trapped, you're in the wrong container. Find a role with movement.

---

## The Element Cycle in Work

Elements interact. A Fire person working in a Water industry (transport, shipping, logistics) will feel drained. A Wood person working in a Metal industry (law, surgery) will feel cut down.

Check your Day Master element. Check your industry's element. If they clash, you'll feel it — no matter how good the salary is.
