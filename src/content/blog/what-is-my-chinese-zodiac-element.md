Your Chinese zodiac sign (Rat, Ox, Tiger, etc.) is only half the story. Each sign is paired with an element that changes every two years.

Here's a simple way to find yours:

**2024-2025 born**: Wood
**2026-2027 born**: Fire
**2028-2029 born**: Earth
**2030-2031 born**: Metal
**2032-2033 born**: Water

Every two years, the element shifts. So someone born in 1988 (Dragon) is an Earth Dragon, while someone born in 2000 (Dragon) is a Metal Dragon — same animal, completely different energy.

Your element changes how your zodiac sign expresses itself. A Metal Dragon is ambitious and sharp. An Earth Dragon is steady and practical. Knowing both completes the picture.

## Element Meanings

**Wood**: Growth, expansion, creativity — the energy of spring
**Fire**: Passion, transformation, visibility — the energy of summer
**Earth**: Stability, nurture, reliability — the center, between seasons
**Metal**: Precision, structure, discipline — the energy of autumn
**Water**: Wisdom, flow, adaptability — the energy of winter
