Most people think Chinese zodiac compatibility is simple: "I'm a Dragon, she's a Rat — we're good, right?" That's like judging a book by its cover. The real system is more nuanced — and more accurate.

There are three main relationship patterns in Chinese zodiac compatibility:

- **Six Harmony (六合)** — The strongest bond
- **Three Harmony (三合)** — Natural alliance
- **Six Clash (六冲)** — Direct opposition

Here's the full breakdown.

---

## The Six Harmonies (六合) — Soulmate Bonds

The Six Harmony pairings are the most compatible in Chinese astrology. These pairs naturally complement each other.

| Your Sign | Best Match | Why It Works |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| Rat | Ox | Rat's creativity meets Ox's stability — perfect balance |
| Ox | Rat | Ox's steadiness gives Rat a secure foundation |
| Tiger | Pig | Tiger's courage + Pig's gentleness = ideal balance |
| Rabbit | Dog | Both value peace, loyalty, and home |
| Dragon | Rooster | Dragon's vision + Rooster's precision = unstoppable |
| Snake | Monkey | Snake's wisdom + Monkey's cleverness = deep understanding |
| Horse | Goat | Horse's drive + Goat's artistry = creative partnership |
| Goat | Horse | Goat needs Horse's forward energy to thrive |
| Monkey | Snake | Monkey's adaptability + Snake's depth = powerful pair |
| Rooster | Dragon | Rooster adds details to Dragon's big picture |
| Dog | Rabbit | Loyalty meets gentleness — a safe, loving bond |
| Pig | Tiger | Pig brings warmth; Tiger brings protection |

---

## The Three Harmonies (三合) — Natural Allies

These are trios of signs that share a common element and support each other.

**Fire Trine**: Tiger, Horse, Dog — passionate, active, independent
**Water Trine**: Rat, Monkey, Dragon — ambitious, strategic, successful
**Metal Trine**: Ox, Rooster, Snake — disciplined, precise, methodical
**Wood Trine**: Rabbit, Goat, Pig — creative, gentle, artistic

If you're in a Three Harmony with someone, you naturally understand each other. Communication is easy. Conflict is manageable.

---

## The Six Clashes (六冲) — Challenging Dynamics

These are direct opposites. They create friction — which can be passionate or destructive, depending on the people involved.

| Pair | Dynamic |
|------|---------|
| Rat vs Horse | Activity (Rat) vs speed (Horse) — competitive energy |
| Ox vs Goat | Stability (Ox) vs flexibility (Goat) — patience tested |
| Tiger vs Monkey | Authority (Tiger) vs cleverness (Monkey) — power struggle |
| Rabbit vs Rooster | Peace (Rabbit) vs precision (Rooster) — sensitivity vs bluntness |
| Dragon vs Dog | Vision (Dragon) vs suspicion (Dog) — trust issues |
| Snake vs Pig | Depth (Snake) vs openness (Pig) — secrecy vs honesty |

Clash pairings aren't doomed. Some of the strongest relationships I've seen are Clash pairs — the friction creates growth. But they require more work.
